
Week 5 - Student Information System

1. Why are the variables declared as private?

The variables are declared as private to protect student information.
Other classes cannot access them directly.

2. What happens if the variables are declared public?

Other classes can access and change the variables directly.
This may cause incorrect data.

3. Why do we use getters and setters?

Getters are used to get the values.
Setters are used to change the values safely.
